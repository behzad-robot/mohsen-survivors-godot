# Product requirements document

**Status:** initial draft, derived from `game-design-notes.md`. **Audience:** developer and artist. This document states the currently approved design brief; **TBD** entries are open design questions, not instructions to invent a rule.

## Product concept

Create a survivors-like game centered on Mohsen, a tech lead facing corporate work as enemies inside a corporate office world. Brotato is the main gameplay reference, with Vampire Survivors as a secondary reference. Reference games guide discussion; specific behavior must be stated in these project docs before it becomes a requirement.

| Shotgun | Use the approved shorter V2 sprite. Fire several pellets in an angular spread for high close-range damage. Each pellet has limited travel and lifetime and stops on its first enemy hit unless Bullet pierce is equipped. Pellet art may reuse the approved player-bullet frames until a separate asset is approved. Pellet count, spread, per-pellet damage, interval, speed, lifetime, and range are provisional editable values. |

| Arena | Top-down 2D rectangular level, slightly larger than the camera view, bounded by walls. |
| Combat | Equipped weapons automatically target the nearest enemy. Their visuals turn quickly but visibly toward an available target, keep their last angle when no target is in range, and do not reset to an idle angle after shooting. Mohsen has no hands. A thrown knife has a finite lifetime and can damage each enemy it crosses once. Gun bullets stop at the first enemy hit by default; unique Saeed adds two further distinct hits per bullet or pellet. Each shot causes a small screen shake; enemies flash fully white and move back slightly when hit. Gun sprites recoil briefly and use the same approved one-frame muzzle flash at their barrel tips when firing. |
| Combat feel | Add distinct sound feedback for weapon shots, enemy hits and defeats, coin pickup, shop purchase, wave start, and Mohsen taking damage. Use brief impact pauses on knife critical hits, boss hits, and enemy defeats while keeping rapid SMG fire fluid. Shortly before the orange boss's first volley in each stopped firing phase, flash its existing sprite red as a warning; then fire the current pattern with its safe gaps. Durations, flash rate, and audio mix are provisional for designer tuning. |
| Game-feel polish 2 | Animate existing spawn warnings through their configured duration; give ordinary ranged enemies a visible pre-shot tell; punch the HUD HP bar and tint screen edges only on actual player damage; distinguish automatic weapon merges in the shop; show brief wave-start and wave-cleared beats without changing active duration; add a HUD coin-count bounce and gentle pickup pitch streak. Keep provisional timings editable. The designer excluded replacement of the current placeholder sound palette. See `dev-feature-requests/task-game-feel-polish-2.md`. |
| Enemy behavior | Start with flexible melee and ranged scene types. Melee follows Mohsen; ranged approaches to a configurable firing distance, shoots, and retreats within a configurable too-close distance. Ranged enemy bullets live long enough to leave the visible screen. The approved orange boss begins slightly slower than Mohsen in wave 1, periodically stops, and fires circular volleys of the same red envelopes with deliberate safe angular gaps. Its vulnerable and contact area should fit its large visible body. Scenes can be duplicated with different sprites. The approved female Jira slime uses exactly the existing melee logic with a different sprite. The approved five-eyed blue slime wanders around the arena instead of following Mohsen, fires eight bullets in a circle, and spawns many approved one-eyed small enemies when it dies. Exact attack timing, offspring count, and offspring behavior/tuning remain provisional. Six approved tank visual variants correspond to the six current enemy sprites and retain their base behavior; tank durability remains provisional. |
| Charge enemy | Use the approved charging paper-shredder sprite at `assets/art/approved/enemies/charge_enemy.png`. Approach Mohsen like a melee enemy. When he enters an editable charge radius, capture his current position once, then charge in a straight line toward that fixed destination without steering as he moves. Stop at the destination, remain still for an editable 3 seconds by default, then resume pursuit. Reuse melee contact damage and shared enemy systems. Charge speed, radius, arrival tolerance, HP, and wave placement are provisional editable developer values. See `_docs/dev-feature-requests/task-charge-enemy.md`. |
| Waves | The full run target is 20 waves of 45 seconds each. The current playable set has editable data for waves 1–10: timed bursts, enemy counts, HP, speed, damage, and knockback-resistance adjustments. Enemy HP follows a per-enemy linear rule: `(base HP + HP gain per wave × (wave number − 1)) × wave danger level HP multiplier`, with an additional existing spawn-group HP multiplier where configured. The wave danger multiplier is editable per wave: waves 1–4 use the 1.0 default; waves 5–10 use 1.2, 1.2, 1.5, 2.0, 2.0, and 3.0 respectively. Future wave resources default to 1.0 until tuned. Each enemy scene owns its editable base and gain values; the current values are provisional mappings for this game's enemies. Tank variants have 2.5× their normal counterpart's base HP and gain, so they remain 2.5× tougher in the same wave. Enemy movement speed rises across waves, reaching at least 2× the wave-1 base by wave 5 or 6; each burst has an editable damage multiplier so enemy contact and hostile projectiles also grow stronger over time. Waves 6–10 add provisional density and tank/boss chances. The orange boss spawns immediately at the start of wave 1 for testing. Approved tank variants have an editable chance to replace their corresponding base enemy starting in wave 3; no tank variants appear in waves 1–2. The tank boss is eligible only at a wave-3-or-later boss spawn. The exact spawn chances and counts are provisional for tuning. The designer expands and tunes waves 11–20 later. |
| Spawn warning | At each burst's configured time t, randomly choose legal spawn positions and show red crosses there. After an Inspector-editable warning duration, 1 second by default, remove the crosses and spawn the enemies at those exact positions. A t=0 burst starts warning at wave start. |
| Drops | Each defeated enemy drops 0/1/2/3 coins with 10%/30%/35%/25% chances. Coins appear with a brief growth animation at slightly scattered positions. A coin entering Mohsen's circular pickup area first springs a short distance away, then accelerates toward him until collected. The area is slightly larger than his body. Coins left on the field at wave end are lost. |
| Shop | Between waves, show four random weapon/item offers and a reroll button; no weapon offer is guaranteed. Each slot chooses a weapon with 40% probability or a passive item with 60% probability. Passive items use the 1.75× rounded-up catalog prices, except later explicit revisions: Behzad costs 80 and Arsalan 95. Current prices, rarities, uniqueness, and wave-based rarity weights are defined in `_docs/items-db.md`. All weapon offer prices are 3× their prior values: tiers I–IV cost 18/30/42/54 coins. The temporary +5% damage sample remains outside the active pool. Reroll prices are unchanged. All item rarities are available from the first shop. Multiple purchases per visit are allowed. Successive rerolls cost 5, 10, 15, 20, etc. |
| Shop slots | Each offer can be locked and stays across rerolls and later shops until unlocked or bought. Bought slots remain empty until a reroll or the next shop. At four equipped weapons, new weapon purchases are blocked; an identical same-tier purchase that can combine into a higher tier remains allowed. |
| Shop inventory | Show equipped weapons and bought items in separate rows at the bottom of the shop. Repeat purchases of the same item display a multiplier. Hovering an entry shows useful current weapon or item stats, including damage and attack speed where applicable. |
| Shop item rarity frames | Rarity 1 item frames keep their current appearance. Rarity 2 item frames use a deep blue background and blue border; rarity 3 uses a deep purple background and purple border. Both backgrounds must work with the shop's light text. Weapon frames remain as before. |
| Shop screen layout | Follow the designer-provided dark shop reference with four offer cards showing icon, name, role/type, description, price, and lock action. Put a coin icon beside each Buy button; place a clickable coin button beside the dark-gray reroll button at top right. Place current coins at top center, a title naming the next wave (or `Last Wave` when none), current stats at right center, Hamidreza below the stats, bought-item and equipped-weapon grids along the bottom, and Start Next Wave at bottom right. Default Godot controls are sufficient; no new art is required. |
| Shopkeeper and speech | Show approved Hamidreza portrait below the stats panel with a speech bubble beside him instead of the prior inline shop message. On the first shop entry of a run, the bubble says exactly `welcome to VX the Greatest function of Snappfood`. Purchase and reroll feedback also appears in the bubble; a short pop/fade animation draws attention whenever a new message is shown. |
| Damage feedback | Each hit on an enemy shows a brief animated damage number with a black outline: white for normal hits and yellow for critical hits. Each accepted hit on Mohsen shows the same floating effect in red with the actual HP lost. Enemy death also shows a punchy fading status phrase selected from CLOSED, RESOLVED, PENDING, CANCELLED, NOT VALID, and NOT ENOUGH INFO. |
| Health | Mohsen starts with 10 HP. Wave-1 baseline enemy hits remove 1 HP; later waves and bursts can increase enemy contact and projectile damage. An accepted hit is followed by an editor-configurable invulnerability period, default 1 second, with sprite flashing. Each full active-wave second adds the nonnegative regeneration value to a fractional accumulator; only its whole-number HP is healed, leaving the fraction for later ticks. No regeneration is banked while at full HP. HP is refilled at the start of each new wave. |
| Stats and items | Initial stats: HP, flat movement speed, knockback, bonus damage %, ranged bonus damage %, melee bonus damage %, HP regeneration per second, critical chance bonus %, Spread, and Bullet pierce. Items can modify these, including tradeoffs; rare items may modify enemies or GameManager flags. Behzad grants +200 percentage points bonus damage and −10 max HP for 80 coins. Arsalan grants +25 percentage points critical chance and −10 max HP for 95 coins; the chance adds to a weapon's base critical chance and actual probability is clamped to 0–100%. Keep the stat/effect model extensible. Store signed HP regeneration but floor effective healing at zero, with no HP drain. Non-unique items repeat and stack additively; a unique item can be owned once per run. Arezoo is unique and grants Spread +3: a player bullet or shotgun pellet that hits an enemy emits three evenly spaced bullets with the original projectile's traits, excluding the original target; spawned bullets never emit more spread bullets. Hossein and Saeed are also unique; their values are in `_docs/items-db.md`. |

Use it as the defeat-screen background and place a separate Play Again control in the center, keeping the characters and baked-in speech unobscured. On 2026-10-12 the designer requested a 16:9 full-screen victory illustration showing happy Mohsen throwing a pile of Jira tickets toward Behzad, Pooria, and Narges, leaving space for a separate Play Again button. The designer approved V1 on 2026-10-12 and requested developer handoff: `assets/art/approved/ui/victory.png` (opaque 1672×941 PNG, approximately 16:9). See `_docs/dev-feature-requests/task-victory-screen.md`; integration and in-game verification are pending. Final title copy remains TBD. |

| Character presentation | Start Mohsen at roughly 55–65 display pixels tall at 1280×720 and ordinary enemies at roughly 45–60; confirm scale in context. Mohsen needs idle and walk only. Code handles hit flashing and death shrinking. Enemy walk may use frames or code-driven scaling; the initial enemy visuals are slightly enlarged and use a more pronounced scale cycle. |

| Initial UI | Default Godot controls are sufficient for Play, weapon choice, shop, and HUD until the designer requests custom UI art. |

Enemy damage uses editable values per enemy: `(BaseDamage + DamagePerWave × (WaveNumber − 1)) × WaveDangerLevelDamageMultiplier` before player defenses. The gain counts from wave 1 even for enemies introduced later. Contact and ranged attack values may differ. The damage danger multiplier is a separate wave setting: waves 1–6 use 1.0, waves 7–10 use 1.2/1.2/2.0/2.2, and future waves default to 1.0. It does not change the HP danger values. Existing per-burst damage factors remain additional optional tuning, currently at their 1.0 defaults. Fractional damage is retained in Mohsen's float HP and floating hit text without rounding. Office-enemy damage mappings are provisional developer values based on the [community Brotato enemy table](https://brotato.wiki.spellsandguns.com/Enemies).

## First playable scope

Designer addition (2026-10-10): spawn 1–3 water dispensers at each wave start except wave 1. Each drops a glass of water every 10 seconds at a random nearby position with an animated drop. Water collection heals 3 HP. Show green floating heal values around Mohsen whenever he heals, including regeneration and water, matching enemy damage-number presentation. Each defeated enemy also has a 10% chance to drop coffee; collection grants +50% of current movement speed for 5 seconds. Approved assets and implementation details are in `_docs/dev-feature-requests/task-water-and-coffee.md`. Repeated coffee pickup behavior and unspecified tuning remain provisional.

Designer addition (2026-10-11): Coffee cups and landed water glasses enter the same circular pickup radius that attracts coins. Once in range, they spring outward briefly, then accelerate toward Mohsen and apply their effect when they reach him, even if he moves away. The radius is controlled by Mohsen's coin pickup radius setting.

Designer addition (2026-10-11): Mania replaces its former HP/knockback effects with +100% projectile size and +100 knockback, plus a restriction that Mohsen cannot shoot while moving. Show `You cant shoot while moving.` in red on the shop card. Its later approved price is 75 coins; rarity and repeatability remain as listed in `_docs/items-db.md`.

Designer addition (2026-10-10): add the purchasable passive item **Strawberry milkshake**, description exactly `item`, granting +10 max HP, rarity 1, at a final cost of 20 coins. Use its approved 48×48 PNG at `assets/art/approved/objects/strawberry_milkshake.png`. Uniqueness remains TBD; record any temporary default as provisional. The database records the exact addition.

The current playable scope is Play -> initial weapon choice -> waves 1–10 in one arena, with the four-offer shop between waves and provisional developer-authored difficulty. The shop offers weapons and the passive catalog now handed off in `_docs/items-db.md`; the temporary +5% damage sample is removed from the active pool. Clearing wave 10 currently shows the approved victory illustration with a separate Play Again button; this remains the playable slice's completion trigger. The eventual run target is 20 waves with victory or defeat screens. The expected full-run triggers are completing wave 20 or reaching 0 HP, respectively; the designer has not explicitly confirmed those transitions. Item balance and final victory title/copy remain subject to designer review. Placeholders can support features while approved art is pending.

The designer's 2026-10-04 direction changes wave duration to 45 seconds and adds the orange bullet-hell boss to wave 1 for testing. This explicitly supersedes the older 65-second statement in the designer-owned `_docs/game-design-notes.md`; the designer can update those notes when ready. Boss health, pattern timing, projectile speed, and its longer-term wave placement remain provisional/TBD pending a designer run.

## Weapon tiers

Knife, pistol, and SMG have tiers I–IV. Use the tier damage, attack interval, and crit data in `tech-features.md`, sourced from the Brotato Wiki, as the initial values. The knife range is now a provisional developer value in response to the designer request for a farther throw; pistol and SMG range remain from the selected reference. Shotgun provisionally follows the same four-tier merge system with developer-authored editable values. The designer later changed projectile hit rules and asked for more knife knockback and less gun knockback; those newer rules supersede the wiki values for those fields. Two identical weapons of the same tier can automatically combine into one weapon of the next tier. A purchase that would combine with an equipped weapon is permitted even when all four slots are occupied. Tier IV has no higher tier; how surplus duplicates are handled is TBD.

## Platform and scripting direction

The current implementation targets native desktop in Godot 4.6 and uses C# for most scripts. The designer has deferred web export for later. A future web version may require an engine or language change; it is outside the current implementation scope.

- Final Mohsen design and scale; individual enemy designs; audio and window scaling.

## Art production order

The artist works one assignment at a time. First create Mohsen from the designer's photos and submit for approval. After Mohsen is approved, create the simple arena floor/background and boundary wall, then wait for further individual tasks. The developer continues with placeholders and swaps only designer-approved art from `assets/art/approved/`.

The artist also maintains the full known backlog in `art-requirements.md`, including the red spawn warning mark. Listed later assets remain deferred until the designer requests them. The developer reads the artist guide and art requirements for coordination while continuing engine work with placeholders.

## Change process

When the designer updates `game-design-notes.md`, reflect explicit decisions here and then revise art and technical handoff docs as needed. Keep unresolved items marked **TBD**. If this document conflicts with the notes, use the notes and flag the mismatch to the designer.
