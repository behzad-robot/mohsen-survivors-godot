# Product requirements document

**Status:** initial draft, derived from `game-design-notes.md`. **Audience:** developer and artist. This document states the currently approved design brief; **TBD** entries are open design questions, not instructions to invent a rule.

## Product concept

Create a survivors-like game centered on Mohsen, a tech lead facing corporate work as enemies inside a corporate office world. Brotato is the main gameplay reference, with Vampire Survivors as a secondary reference. Reference games guide discussion; specific behavior must be stated in these project docs before it becomes a requirement.

## Confirmed requirements

| Area | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Player | One playable character named Mohsen, presented as a tech lead. |
| Equipment | Plan for approximately 3–6 weapon types overall. Knife, pistol, SMG, and shotgun are confirmed; the player may equip at most four weapons. The original three types have four tiers; shotgun uses provisional tiers I–IV within the existing merge system pending designer balance review. |
| Items | The designer handed off `_docs/items-db.md` as the current purchasable passive catalog. Its listed modifiers, costs, rarities, and uniqueness are editable starting values for implementation and later designer balance review. The temporary +5% damage sample is removed from the active pool. Fourteen teammate portraits are approved under `assets/art/approved/items/teammates/`; a shared YAML icon is approved for DevOps items, and Hookah, Propranolol pills, and Heavy Smoker V2 have approved icons. Approved paths are in `art-requirements.md`; other entries use developer placeholders. Heavy Smoker's role is Trait. |
| Enemies | Include corporate work personified as enemies, specifically initiatives and Jira tickets. Other types are TBD. |
| Setting | Corporate office theme for the world and visual assets. |
| Production | Gameplay can use simple placeholders until corresponding artwork is ready and designer-approved. |
| Platform | Target the current native desktop Godot project. Web export is deferred; mobile is out of scope for now. |
| Display | Use a 1280×720 (16:9) design view for native desktop. Window scaling behavior is TBD. |
| Movement input | Support both WASD and arrow keys. Mohsen faces left while moving left, including diagonals, and faces right while moving right; vertical-only movement retains the last horizontal facing. |
| Starting choice | Before play begins, the player chooses exactly one starting weapon: knife, pistol, SMG, or shotgun. |
| Knife | A thrown weapon with a finite lifetime and a white flight trail. One throw can hit multiple enemies, but each enemy at most once. The designer requested a farther, faster throw with eased speed; the developer chooses provisional range and speed values for testing. Keep the selected tier damage, interval, and crit values as starting balance; knockback is higher than the guns. |
| Pistol and SMG | Available automatic starting weapons. Their bullets stop on the first enemy hit by default; Saeed lets them pierce two additional distinct enemies. Hits show the four-frame impact effect extracted from the designer's 2026-10-05 reference. Both bullet scenes use the same two-frame one-shot visual animation from the reference; it holds on the second frame. Keep the selected tier damage, interval, range, and crit values as starting balance; gun knockback is lower than knife knockback. |
| Shotgun | Use the approved shorter V2 sprite. Fire several pellets in an angular spread for high close-range damage. Each pellet has limited travel and lifetime and stops on its first enemy hit unless Bullet pierce is equipped. Pellet art may reuse the approved player-bullet frames until a separate asset is approved. Pellet count, spread, per-pellet damage, interval, speed, lifetime, and range are provisional editable values. |
| Game flow | Play button -> choose one initial weapon -> wave 1 -> shop -> later waves. One environment is sufficient for the first playable version. |
| Arena | Top-down 2D rectangular level, slightly larger than the camera view, bounded by walls. |
| Combat | Equipped weapons automatically target the nearest enemy. Mohsen has no hands. A thrown knife has a finite lifetime and can damage each enemy it crosses once. Gun bullets stop at the first enemy hit by default; Saeed adds two further distinct hits per bullet or pellet, stacking additively across purchases. Each shot causes a small screen shake; enemies flash fully white and move back slightly when hit. Gun sprites recoil briefly and use the same approved one-frame muzzle flash at their barrel tips when firing. |
| Combat feel | Add distinct sound feedback for weapon shots, enemy hits and defeats, coin pickup, shop purchase, wave start, and Mohsen taking damage. Use brief impact pauses on knife critical hits, boss hits, and enemy defeats while keeping rapid SMG fire fluid. Shortly before the orange boss's first volley in each stopped firing phase, flash its existing sprite red as a warning; then fire the current pattern with its safe gaps. Durations, flash rate, and audio mix are provisional for designer tuning. |
| Enemy behavior | Start with flexible melee and ranged scene types. Melee follows Mohsen; ranged approaches to a configurable firing distance, shoots, and retreats within a configurable too-close distance. Ranged enemy bullets live long enough to leave the visible screen. The approved orange boss moves slightly slower than Mohsen, periodically stops, and fires circular volleys of the same red envelopes with deliberate safe angular gaps. Its vulnerable and contact area should fit its large visible body. Scenes can be duplicated with different sprites. The approved female Jira slime uses exactly the existing melee logic with a different sprite. The approved five-eyed blue slime wanders around the arena instead of following Mohsen, fires eight bullets in a circle, and spawns many approved one-eyed small enemies when it dies. Exact attack timing, offspring count, and offspring behavior/tuning remain provisional. Six approved tank visual variants correspond to the six current enemy sprites and retain their base behavior; tank durability remains provisional. |
| Waves | The full run target is 20 waves of 45 seconds each. For the first playable version, the developer authors editable starting data for waves 1–5: timed bursts, enemy counts, HP, speed, and knockback-resistance adjustments. The orange boss spawns immediately at the start of wave 1 for testing. Approved tank variants have an editable chance to replace their corresponding base enemy starting in wave 3; no tank variants appear in waves 1–2. The tank boss is eligible only at a wave-3-or-later boss spawn. The exact chance and durability values are provisional for tuning. The designer expands and tunes the rest later. |
| Spawn warning | At each burst's configured time t, randomly choose legal spawn positions and show red crosses there. After an Inspector-editable warning duration, 1 second by default, remove the crosses and spawn the enemies at those exact positions. A t=0 burst starts warning at wave start. |
| Drops | Each defeated enemy drops 0/1/2/3 coins with 10%/30%/35%/25% chances. Coins appear with a brief growth animation at slightly scattered positions. A coin entering Mohsen's circular pickup area first springs a short distance away, then accelerates toward him until collected. The area is slightly larger than his body. Coins left on the field at wave end are lost. |
| Shop | Between waves, show four random weapon/item offers and a reroll button; no weapon offer is guaranteed. Each slot chooses a weapon with 40% probability or a passive item with 60% probability. The active passive catalog, prices, rarities, uniqueness, and wave-based rarity weights are defined in `_docs/items-db.md`; remove the temporary +5% damage sample from the pool. All item rarities are available from the first shop. Multiple purchases per visit are allowed. Successive rerolls cost 5, 10, 15, 20, etc. |
| Shop slots | Each offer can be locked and stays across rerolls and later shops until unlocked or bought. Bought slots remain empty until a reroll or the next shop. At four equipped weapons, new weapon purchases are blocked; an identical same-tier purchase that can combine into a higher tier remains allowed. |
| Shop inventory | Show equipped weapons and bought items in separate rows at the bottom of the shop. Repeat purchases of the same item display a multiplier. Hovering an entry shows useful current weapon or item stats, including damage and attack speed where applicable. |
| Shop screen layout | Follow the designer-provided dark shop reference with four offer cards showing icon, name, role/type, description, price, and lock action. Place current coins at top center, reroll at top right, a title naming the next wave (or `Last Wave` when none), current stats at right center, Hamidreza below the stats, bought-item and equipped-weapon grids along the bottom, and Start Next Wave at bottom right. Default Godot controls are sufficient; no new art is required. |
| Shopkeeper and speech | Show approved Hamidreza portrait below the stats panel with a speech bubble beside him instead of the prior inline shop message. On the first shop entry of a run, the bubble says exactly `welcome to VX the Greatest function of Snappfood`. Purchase and reroll feedback also appears in the bubble. |
| Damage feedback | Each hit on an enemy shows a brief animated white damage number with a black outline. Enemy death also shows a punchy fading status phrase selected from CLOSED, RESOLVED, PENDING, CANCELLED, NOT VALID, and NOT ENOUGH INFO. |
| Health | Mohsen starts with 10 HP. Each enemy hit removes 1 HP, followed by an editor-configurable invulnerability period, default 1 second, with sprite flashing. HP regeneration occurs during waves; HP is refilled at the start of each new wave. |
| Stats and items | Initial stats: HP, flat movement speed, knockback, bonus damage %, ranged bonus damage %, melee bonus damage %, and HP regeneration per second, Spread, and Bullet pierce. Items can modify these, including tradeoffs; rare items may modify enemies or GameManager flags. Keep the stat/effect model extensible. Store signed HP regeneration but floor effective healing at zero, with no HP drain. Non-unique items repeat and stack additively; a unique item can be owned once per run. Arezoo is unique and grants Spread +3: a player bullet or shotgun pellet that hits an enemy emits three evenly spaced bullets with the original projectile's traits, excluding the original target; spawned bullets never emit more spread bullets. Hossein and Saeed use the latest values in `_docs/items-db.md`. |
| End screens | Victory and defeat have separate screens with the same title, image, Play Again button structure. Their title text and images differ. |
| Art direction | Use the simple, bold, readable cartoon feel of Brotato and The Binding of Isaac as references. Mohsen is a distinct human tech lead based on designer-provided photos. Saved gameplay screenshots guide scale and style; they are not in-game assets. |
| Character presentation | Start Mohsen at roughly 55–65 display pixels tall at 1280×720 and ordinary enemies at roughly 45–60; confirm scale in context. Mohsen needs idle and walk only. Code handles hit flashing and death shrinking. Enemy walk may use frames or code-driven scaling; the initial enemy visuals are slightly enlarged and use a more pronounced scale cycle. |
| Environment art | The first arena only needs one floor/background and boundary walls. No decorative or blocking props are required now. |
| Initial UI | Default Godot controls are sufficient for Play, weapon choice, shop, and HUD until the designer requests custom UI art. |
| Editor testing shortcuts | Only while playing from the Godot editor: `C` grants 100 coins, including in the shop; pressing `P` in the shop freely rerolls all four offers to eligible Product Manager items only; `R` restarts the game. `P` no longer ends a wave. These shortcuts are absent from exported builds. |
| During-wave HUD | Show a large top-center timer, yellow below 20 seconds and red below 10; place the wave name above it as `Wave-{X}-{Wave Name}` or `Wave-{X}` when unnamed. Show white `{current}/{max}` HP text inside a red bar on black, with a coin icon and count beneath it at top left. Wave names 1–5 are Onboarding, Vendor-Automation-Nightmare, Dakhl-Access, Vendor-Automation-Decomposition, and Vendor Own Delivery. |
| Minor combat improvements | Show a small red-on-black health bar over Mohsen only while below full HP. Shorten enemies' white hit flash. Every ordinary spawn burst in waves 1–5 contains at least ten enemies; raise ordinary enemy speed while keeping it below Mohsen's starting speed. For wave 1, set default ordinary melee HP near 10 and ranged HP near 15; these are designer-directed tuning targets. Give each enemy damage number a small random spawn offset. Other exact values remain provisional for designer tuning. |
| Minor updates 2 | Mix male and female Jira visuals within wave melee groups. The many-eyes enemy fires the approved red envelopes. Do not offer an item if its HP penalty would leave Mohsen at zero or lower HP. In shop item descriptions, show positive effects in green and negative effects in red. |

## First playable scope

The first playable scope is Play -> initial weapon choice -> waves 1–5 in one arena, with the four-offer shop between waves and provisional developer-authored difficulty. The shop offers weapons and the passive catalog now handed off in `_docs/items-db.md`; the temporary +5% damage sample is removed from the active pool. The post-wave-5 state may be provisional and should be reported for designer review. The eventual run target is 20 waves with victory or defeat screens. The expected full-run triggers are completing wave 20 or reaching 0 HP, respectively; the designer has not explicitly confirmed those transitions. Item balance and finish-screen copy remain subject to designer review. The developer should receive a specific feature task before implementation; placeholders can support features while approved art is pending.

The designer's 2026-10-04 direction changes wave duration to 45 seconds and adds the orange bullet-hell boss to wave 1 for testing. This explicitly supersedes the older 65-second statement in the designer-owned `_docs/game-design-notes.md`; the designer can update those notes when ready. Boss health, pattern timing, projectile speed, and its longer-term wave placement remain provisional/TBD pending a designer run.

## Weapon tiers

Knife, pistol, and SMG have tiers I–IV. Use the tier damage, attack interval, and crit data in `tech-features.md`, sourced from the Brotato Wiki, as the initial values. The knife range is now a provisional developer value in response to the designer request for a farther throw; pistol and SMG range remain from the selected reference. Shotgun provisionally follows the same four-tier merge system with developer-authored editable values. The designer later changed projectile hit rules and asked for more knife knockback and less gun knockback; those newer rules supersede the wiki values for those fields. Two identical weapons of the same tier can automatically combine into one weapon of the next tier. A purchase that would combine with an equipped weapon is permitted even when all four slots are occupied. Tier IV has no higher tier; how surplus duplicates are handled is TBD.

## Platform and scripting direction

The current implementation targets native desktop in Godot 4.6 and uses C# for most scripts. The designer has deferred web export for later. A future web version may require an engine or language change; it is outside the current implementation scope.

## Design details awaiting decisions

- How weapon stat scaling from the wiki maps to percentage-based player stats; final projectile speed and lifetime tuning; duplicate behavior when slots remain or a weapon is tier IV.
- Final tuning of the item prices, rarity weights, and stat effects in `_docs/items-db.md` after designer playtesting; item tiers are not currently defined.
- Whether the reroll cost sequence resets each shop visit and whether coins carry between shops; the current implementation's choices remain provisional.
- Final data for waves 6–20 and tuning feedback on developer-authored waves 1–5. Enemy appearances and their behavior mapping are deferred until individual art tasks.
- Exact victory/defeat title text and artwork, defeat transition timing, and Play Again behavior.
- Final Mohsen design and scale; individual enemy designs; audio and window scaling.

## Art production order

The artist works one assignment at a time. First create Mohsen from the designer's photos and submit for approval. After Mohsen is approved, create the simple arena floor/background and boundary wall, then wait for further individual tasks. The developer continues with placeholders and swaps only designer-approved art from `assets/art/approved/`.

The artist also maintains the full known backlog in `art-requirements.md`, including the red spawn warning mark. Listed later assets remain deferred until the designer requests them. The developer reads the artist guide and art requirements for coordination while continuing engine work with placeholders.

## Change process

When the designer updates `game-design-notes.md`, reflect explicit decisions here and then revise art and technical handoff docs as needed. Keep unresolved items marked **TBD**. If this document conflicts with the notes, use the notes and flag the mismatch to the designer.
