# Six item icons — V1 art briefs

All six are original centered inventory illustrations on transparent square canvases. Use bold near-black outlines, simplified polished cartoon cel shading, saturated colors, no text, no border, and silhouettes readable at 128×128. The 128×128 candidates are cropped to visible alpha, proportionally scaled into a 114×114 area, and centered with transparent margins. Full-resolution image generation outputs are the matching `*_concept_v1.png` files.

- **Pizza:** Whole pepperoni pizza from a slightly elevated three-quarter view, with one slice pulled out, golden crust, cheese, tomato sauce, and sparse green herb accents.
- **Hamburger:** Sesame bun with distinct lettuce, tomato, cheese, and beef patty layers, slightly elevated three-quarter view.
- **Team Building:** Two hands firmly clasped in mutual support, with blue and teal office sleeves. Exactly two people implied, no other symbol.
- **Tea:** White ceramic cup and saucer, amber tea, a visible tea bag string and plain blue tag, one small steam curl.
- **Beach Straw Hat:** Wide-brimmed light golden woven straw hat with a sky-blue ribbon, three-quarter view.
- **Apple:** Glossy red apple with short brown stem, a green leaf, and a small highlight.
